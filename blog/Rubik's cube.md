# Rubik's cube

I've been cubing a lot recently and I'm currently transitioning from using the 2-look PLL method to full PLL. It's 21 algorithms so that's a lot to keep track of but I'm making good progress. I really like J perms and A perms and the F perm :p 

I also got a new record of 35.71 seconds, so my times are definitely improving. A lot of high 30s and low 40s so my average is going down as well. I just have to keep practicing now!
